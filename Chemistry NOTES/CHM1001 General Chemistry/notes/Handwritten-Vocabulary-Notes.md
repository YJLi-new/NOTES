## L1 — Light, quanta, Bohr model

- velocity (n.) 速率; nature (n.) 本质
- **Planck's formula**: $\rho(\nu, T) = \frac{8\pi h}{c^3}\cdot\frac{\nu^3}{e^{h\nu/kT} - 1}$
- **The hydrogen spectrum**: $\frac1\lambda = R_H\left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)$
- **The Bohr model**: $\Delta E = -hcR_H\left(\frac{1}{n_f^2} - \frac{1}{n_i^2}\right)$ — the energy absorbed/emitted when moving from the initial to the final energy level (e.g. $n = 2 \to 1$: $\Delta E < 0$; $n = 1 \to 2$: $\Delta E > 0$)
- quanta (pl.) 量子 (singular: quantum); photoelectric (adj.) 光电的; spectrum; photon
- excited states 激发态; ground states 基态; integer; corresponding; radiate; nucleus (n.) 原子核; discrete; correspond; transition (n.) 转变, 跃迁; proportional (adj.) 成比例的; solvent (n.) 溶剂; tumor (n.) 肿瘤; paradigm (n.) 范例
- reductionism (n.) 简化论; interpret (v.) 诠释; respectively (adv.) 分别地; infrared (adj.) 红外线的; region (n.) 地区, 区域, 领域
- the wave nature of light 光的波动性; the nature of energy 能量的 (本质?)
- photoelectric effect; photon; work function 逸出功 (功函数); dual wave–particle nature 波粒二象性
- spectrum (n.) 光谱; 频谱; monochromatic (adj.) 单色的; polychromatic (adj.) 多色的; continuous spectrum 连续光谱; line spectrum 线状光谱
- (W2 P27) peak (n.) 波峰; trough (n.) 波谷; periodic ($T$) 周期; $c = \lambda\nu$; quantum (n.) 量子; $E = nh\nu$ ($n$: number of electrons/photons); electromagnetic spectrum
- **Bohr's model**: the energies corresponding to the allowed orbits for the electron in the hydrogen atom:
  $E = (-hcR_H)\left(\frac{1}{n^2}\right)$; $\Delta E = (-2.18\times10^{-18}\,\text{J})\left(\frac{1}{n_f^2} - \frac{1}{n_i^2}\right)$
- ground state 基态; excited state 激发态; the energy change

## Quantum mechanics & electron configuration

- momentum 动量: $p = mv$; matter waves 物质波: $\lambda = \frac{h}{mv}$
- uncertainty principle 测不准原理: $\Delta x\cdot\Delta(mv) \ge \frac{h}{4\pi}$ ($\Delta x$: 位移, $\Delta(mv)$: 动量)
- wave function 波函数: a series of mathematical (functions …)
- probability density (electron density): $\psi^2$
- orbital
- the principal quantum number ($n$) 主量子数 ⇒ 能层 (energy level), $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$
- the angular momentum quantum number ($l$) 角动量量子数 ⇒ 能级; shape (of orbital)
- the magnetic quantum number ($m_l$) 磁量子数 ⇒ 轨道方向
- electron spin 电子自旋; the spin magnetic quantum number ($m_s$) 自旋磁量子数: $-\frac12$ or $+\frac12$
- electron configuration 电子排布; orbital diagram 轨道图 (电子排布图); the Pauli exclusion principle 泡利不相容原理; Hund's rule
- Electromagnetic spectrum (wavelength increases / frequency decreases →): gamma rays | X-rays | ultra-violet 紫外线 | visible | infrared 红外线 | microwaves | radio frequency
