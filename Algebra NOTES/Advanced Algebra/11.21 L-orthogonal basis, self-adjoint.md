**Prop.** $\dim(V) < \infty$, $B$ is an **orthogonal** basis of $(V, \langle\,,\rangle)$ (对不 orthogonal 的 basis 不成立), $T: V \to V$. Then

$$\underbrace{T^*_{BB}}_{\text{adj. op.}} = \overline{(T_{BB})}^t\quad(\text{conjugate transpose})$$

*Proof.* Let $T^*_{BB} = (b_{ij})$, $T_{BB} = (a_{ij})$, $B = \{e_1,\dots,e_n\}$. Then $\langle T^*(e_k), e_l\rangle = \langle e_k, T(e_l)\rangle$ (def. of $T^*$); expanding both sides (见 …) gives $\bar b_{lk} = a_{kl} \iff b_{lk} = \bar a_{kl}$. Hence $T^*_{BB} = \overline{T_{BB}}^t$.

## Self-adjoint Operator

**Def.** $T$ is self-adjoint $\iff T^* = T$.

- **E.g. 1.** $V = \mathbb{R}^n$, $\langle\,,\rangle$ the usual dot product, $T(x) = Ax$. Then $T^*(x) = A^tx$, so $T$ is self-adj. $\iff A = A^t \iff A$ is a symmetric matrix.
- **E.g. 2.** $V = \mathbb{C}^n$, $\langle\,,\rangle$ the usual dot product, $T(x) = Ax$. Then $T$ is self-adj. $\iff \bar A^t = A$.

**Prop. 1.** All eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator are real.

*Proof.* Let $v \in V$ be any eigenvector of $T: V \to V$ with eigenvalue $\lambda$, and $T$ self-adj. Consider $\langle T(v), v\rangle = \langle T^*(v), v\rangle$:

- LHS $= \langle\lambda v, v\rangle = \bar\lambda\langle v, v\rangle$;
- RHS $= \langle v, T(v)\rangle = \langle v, \lambda v\rangle = \lambda\langle v, v\rangle$.

$\Rightarrow \bar\lambda = \lambda \Rightarrow \lambda$ is real.

**Prop. 3 (★ important).** $T: V \to V$ is self-adj. Suppose $U \le V$ is $T$-invariant. Then $U^\perp \le V$ is $T$-invariant.

*Proof.* Let $\forall w \in U^\perp$ (WTS: $T(w) \in U^\perp$). For $u \in U$, $T(u) \in U$. Consider

$$\langle T(w), u\rangle = \langle T^*(w), u\rangle\ (T \text{ self-adj.}) = \langle\underbrace{w}_{\in U^\perp}, \underbrace{T(u)}_{\in U}\rangle = 0\ \Rightarrow\ T(w) \in U^\perp$$
