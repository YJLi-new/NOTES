## Vector Space

**Def.** Let $\mathbb{F}$ be a field ($\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{R}$ real numbers or $\mathbb{C}$ complex numbers).

A **vector space over $\mathbb{F}$** is a set $V$ equipped with:

1. scalar multiplication $\mathbb{F} \times V \to V$, $(\alpha, v) \mapsto \alpha\cdot v$
2. vector addition $V \times V \to V$, $(v, w) \mapsto v + w$

s.t. $\cdot$ and $+$ satisfy the 8 axioms (见于 2040).

**e.g.**

1. $V = \mathbb{R}^n$ is a vector space over $\mathbb{F} = \mathbb{R}$ (likewise $V = \mathbb{F}^n$ over $\mathbb{F}$):

   $$\begin{pmatrix}a_1\\ \vdots\\ a_n\end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix}b_1\\ \vdots\\ b_n\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}a_1+b_1\\ \vdots\\ a_n+b_n\end{pmatrix},\qquad \alpha\begin{pmatrix}a_1\\ \vdots\\ a_n\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}\alpha a_1\\ \vdots\\ \alpha a_n\end{pmatrix}$$

   $\mathbf{0} = (0,\dots,0)^T$; $V = \mathbb{C}^n$ over $\mathbb{R}$ also works.
2. $V = M_{m\times n}(\mathbb{F})$ is a vector space over $\mathbb{F}$:

   $$\alpha\begin{pmatrix}a_{11}&\cdots&a_{1n}\\ \vdots&&\vdots\\ a_{m1}&\cdots&a_{mn}\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}\alpha a_{11}&\cdots\\ \vdots&\end{pmatrix},\qquad (a_{ij}) + (b_{ij}) = (a_{ij}+b_{ij})$$
3. $V = C^\infty(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is a vector space over $\mathbb{R}$ (or 仅: $C_c(\mathbb{R}^n)$, all continuous functions $f:\mathbb{R}^n\to\mathbb{R}$) … (未)
4. $V = P(\mathbb{R})$ (or 仅 $\mathbb{R}[x]$): all polynomials with real coefficients of degree $\le n$ ($\deg(\text{polynomial}) =$ degree of …) … (未)
   - zero vector: $\mathbf{0} = 0 + 0x + \cdots + 0x^n$

## Vector Subspace

**Def.** Let $V$ be a vector space over $\mathbb{F}$. A **vector subspace** of $V$ is a subset $W \subseteq V$ if $W$ is a vector space using the operations $\cdot$ and $+$ in $V$.

**e.g.**

1. $V = \mathbb{R}^2$, $W =$ (a sketched line/region through the origin)
2. $V = \mathbb{R}^2$, $W_2 =$ the first quadrant:

   $(-3)\cdot\begin{pmatrix}1\\2\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}-3\\-6\end{pmatrix} \notin W_2$; 而 $\begin{pmatrix}2\\3\end{pmatrix} + \begin{pmatrix}-1\\-5\end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix}1\\-2\end{pmatrix} \notin W_2$

   $\Rightarrow W_2$ is not a vector subspace of $V$.
