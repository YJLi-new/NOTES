**Enemy design**: Pursuer can move and only attacks with melee weapons; Mortar cannot move and only attacks with thrown objects. Both of these enemies also persist unless the player kills them. Therefore, I came up with the idea for the new enemy Arrow, which can move and will explode and disappear on its own. It should:

Movement: stationary (default) / pursue player (always) (move faster than Pursuer and player); Attack: collide with player (more damage than Pursuer); Injuries: collision (including collision with player and scene) (instant death) / disappear after a certain period of time; Others: It should cause a small explosion when colliding with other Actors, and the explosion should knock back and reduce the player's health at the time of the collision

**Level Design**: The player is born on an island and there is only one way to go. There is a collectible on the way, and the player's score goes up after picking it up, so the player learns that collectibles can be picked up and increase the score. Continuing on, the player sees another conspicuous collectible on the stairs, so naturally the player walks over to it.

On the way to the stairs, the player picks up a health pack, and learns that health packs can also be picked up and are different from collectibles. Arriving at the top of the stairs, the player jumps down through a hole. After jumping, the player steps on the Pursuer's head below, realising that stepping on enemies' heads can destroy them. Continuing on, the player sees a large island in front of them, with a collectible waiting to be picked up. There is no path ahead, and the player sees a button, realising that pressing it will lead to the target island.

Stepping on the button, the player is teleported to an unfamiliar area at the back of the island. Not having collected the collectible yet, the player starts exploring, possibly going up the stairs first, and then seeing the Pursuer. The player may jump down to try to step on the Pursuer's head, but may fail, and the Pursuer will notice and start chasing the player around the island.

Using the terrain, the player destroys the Pursuer or collects the collectible in the chase. At the location of the collectible, the player sees the previously hidden part of the island. There is only one destination here, and the player will go there. Since the Pursuer cannot jump, it may fall out of the world if it is still alive and chasing the player.

Therefore, the player should be safe on this island, but another enemy has emerged here. So here, the player will meet the type of enemy Mortar, but does not have to deal with it. At the same time, the player can also discover on this island that there is another island below as a destination.

This second island is hidden under the original path, giving the player a sense of discovery and freshness in exploration. At the same time, just like Pursuer, the functions of Mortar are gradually revealed here. First, there is a single Mortar display, and then the island below is a whole challenge based on Mortar.

On the third island, the player clearly faces Mortar and needs to use their understanding of the Mortar mechanism. At the same time, a shortcut creates a sense of choice for the player. After passing this challenge, the player will need to redo the challenge with a more difficult path in order to get an additional reward, enhancing the sense of choice and challenge.

On the fourth island, Arrow is shown for the first time. Because of the Mortar blocking it off, and the lure of the collectible reward, the player chooses to be seen by Arrow and chased after. Arrow is faster than the player, but the player has the advantage of not being caught because Arrow turns slowly. So there are lots of obstacles here to help the player get around and escape Arrow. The fifth challenge is the same, but here the characteristics of the three enemies are combined, and the option of a detour route is provided.

At the very end, a very conspicuous huge green button announces the location of victory. The player only needs to jump on it during the exciting chase to win.
