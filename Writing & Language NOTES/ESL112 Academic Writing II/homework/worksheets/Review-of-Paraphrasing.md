**Remember the qualities of a good paraphrase:**

- They use different words and a different structure than the original.

- They do not change the overall meaning of the original.

- They include a source citation.

<p>Heavy use of social media by children has been linked to lower levels of literacy for the first time. The landmark study by University College London (UCL), based on 11,000 children tracked from their births in 2000, found their time on social media could be detracting from reading and homework, with a potential knock-on effect on their literacy. **Your Paraphrased Version**

A study by University College London (UCL) shows that heavy use of social media by children can reduce their literacy levels by distracting them from reading and homework, which may improve their literacy levels, regardless of gender. It also increases the probability of depression in children, especially girls. Therefore, Professor Kelly suggests that the government should consider setting time limits for children to use social media outside of school.
