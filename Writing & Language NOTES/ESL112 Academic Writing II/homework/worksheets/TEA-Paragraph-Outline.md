**Step 3**

Based on the outline you created, write a body paragraph for your problem analysis essay. If time permits, you will start writing the body paragraph in class. Otherwise, write the body paragraph as part of your homework assignment.

|  |
|----|
| One of the main reasons for the food bank shortage crisis in Germany is inflation. The increase in inflation has caused a sharp rise in the cost of living and food prices, making it difficult for more and more low-income people and working families to maintain their basic lives and forced to rely on food banks for food assistance. According to the German Federal Statistical Office, food prices have risen by nearly 15% compared with last year, and the inflation rate is as high as 7.3%, which has greatly increased the food expenditure burden of ordinary families. In addition, the supply chain disruption caused by the war in Ukraine has further pushed up food prices and exacerbated the problem of food shortages. The sharp rise in food prices means that even working families have difficulty affording basic food expenses, forcing them to turn to food banks for help. However, the surge in demand has made the resources of food banks extremely tight, and many food banks have begun to ration food and even have to reject new applicants. This not only affects the poor groups who originally need assistance the most, but also further widens the gap between the rich and the poor in society. If effective policy intervention is not taken, inflation will continue to worsen Germany's food insecurity problem and put more families in a survival dilemma. |
