# Mini AI Disclosure Statement Practice

## Activity

1.  Write an AI disclosure statement using the following information:

    1.  AI used: Claude Anthropic (claude.ai/new)

    2.  Date & time: 2/4/2025 at 12:00 pm

    3.  Used for: Brainstorming appropriate topics for Problem Analysis Essay, and help with distinguishing the causes and effects of the problem

> **Your AI Disclosure Statement:** I used Claude Anthropic with access link claude.ai/new on 2/4/2025 at 12:00 pm for brainstorming appropriate topics for Problem Analysis Essay. I also used it to help with distinguishing the causes and effects of the problem. All AI-generated content was critically reviewed and revised by me to ensure accuracy and relevance.

2.  Write an AI disclosure statement using the following information:

    1.  AI used: Grammarly

    2.  Date & time: 2/4/2025 at 2:00 pm

    3.  Used for: identifying and correcting errors of grammar of the entire Problem Analysis Essay 1<sup>st</sup> Draft

> **Your AI Disclosure Statement:** I used Grammarly on 2/4/2025 at 2:00 pm for identifying and correcting errors of grammar of the entire Problem Analysis Essay 1<sup>st</sup> Draft. All AI-generated content was critically reviewed and revised by me to ensure accuracy and relevance.
