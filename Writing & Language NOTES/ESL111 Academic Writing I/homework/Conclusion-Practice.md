In conclusion, the requirement of wearing uniform makes students can focus more on their schoolwork, also promotes equality in academic settings. As we stated previously, having no choice of what to wear students who can only wear school uniforms are able to allocate more of the time to their studies; the same clothes type among students can also reduce the differences in students' perception of other people's appearance, so the disruption to learning immersion caused by visual stimulation is also reduced. In addition, because the clothes that students wear all look the same, they no longer envy each other over whether their clothes are fashionable or not, and the phenomenon that teachers may have higher expectations for students who dress better is also eliminated totally. These make the students and teachers capable of evaluating each other more objectively, so they can promote equality in the academic atmosphere.
