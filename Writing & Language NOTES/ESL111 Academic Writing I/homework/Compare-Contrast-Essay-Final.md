Remote work, which had to be promoted on a large scale due to epidemic prevention measures during the severe phase of the COVID-19 pandemic, has gradually become a popular work pattern in the United States and will continue to increase, as predicted by the data scientists from Ladders (McDonald, 2021). As a professional who just graduated from college, when looking for a job, the choice of remote work or in-office work is quite important to me due to the fundamental difference in their working locations. They may lead to various choices in renting, transportation, and so on. However, there are different opinions on how the decisions can be made. Some people believe that working in the office can promote communication in work, while others think that remote work improves working efficiency. From my point of view, full-time remote work would be the better option for me. That is because working remotely can improve my work efficiency, make me physically healthier, and make it easier for me to achieve a work-life balance.

Firstly, by boosting productivity and preventing burnout, remote work can really improve my experience in work. This is mainly because working remotely helps me maintain my mental health. When I work in the office, I will have to deal with a lot of additional and unnecessary stress. It is usually brought by competition from my colleagues and supervision by the managers. But if I am not in an office, I will not only be free from this kind of stress but also be more productive by choosing my preferred working location. Like sunshine and greenery in the park, or casual music and talking people in a Café, those things absent in offices will comfort me, calm me down, and concentrate me more on my work. A well-known study shows that the efficiency improvement effect in remote work does exist: the call center employees of Ctrip who work at home have a 13% more performance increase than those who work at the office (Bloom et al., 2015). Obviously, remote work significantly enhances my mental well-being and productivity at work.

Furthermore, working remotely allows me to have a healthier physical body. By choosing to work remotely, there will be more chances for everyday exercise for me. No longer restricted to the office and fixed schedules, I have more flexible time for physical activities. At the same time, my working style becomes more adaptable – I can work while walking around occasionally, or reflect on matter about my work while being outdoors. Moreover, remote work offers benefits that go beyond improving my physical health – it is also safeguarding my health, for instance, by reducing my risk of contracting infectious diseases. With my weakened immune system after recovering from COVID-19, being infected by them could potentially cause irreversible and lifelong harm to my body. Offices are typically crowded and carry a high risk of spreading infectious diseases - as Zavitsanos (2021) stated, some employees at Houston law firms contracted COVID-19 soon after they reopened their offices. Choosing to work remotely full-time offers me the chance to stay away from crowded places like offices, reducing my risk of contracting infectious diseases.

Finally, working remotely allows me to achieve a better work-life balance more easily. Since work consumes half or more of my waking hours, reducing my commute time to almost nothing saves much time. The “additional” time can be used to develop my hobbies or accompany my family, investing more in life outside the workplace. Although working in the office enables me to build relationships with colleagues and enjoy company benefits, it limits my social circle to coworkers and leaders. By working remotely, I would be able to connect with more people outside of work and paying greater attention to relationships beyond the office.

In conclusion, working out of the office brings many benefits to me. I will have better work experience as the stress is lower and my working location can be chosen to be relaxing. I will have a healthier body as I have more free time and my working location becomes flexible for me to exercise more. I will also achieve a better work-life balance as I can spare more time to my families and hobbies and connect with more people beyond the office. Consequently, for me, full-time remote work must be the better option when looking for a job compared to office-based work. For others, remote work can reduce traffic problems and environmental pollution caused by commuting, and it increases the pool of talent available to companies from various areas. I believe that remote work has the potential to transform the entire society.
