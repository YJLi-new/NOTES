1\. What is the main argument of the essay?:

Private housing has advantages in privacy and price than university housing.

2\. Which two features (Points of Comparison) about private housing and university housing does this essay compare?

PoC1: Private housing keeps more privacy than university housing

PoC2: Private housing may cost less than university housing

3\. What method of organization does the writer use?—point-by-point or block?:

Point-by-point. The writer firstly present an introduction paragraph, then explain a point of comparison in each body paragraph.

4\. What evidence/arguments does the writer provide to support PoC1?:

Most private housing have options offer private bedrooms and restrooms, but university housing often requires sharing rooms and restroom with other students.

5\. What evidence/arguments does the writer provide to support PoC2?:

The dorms (of private housing) often require students to purchase meal plans. At the same time, private housing may charge additional fees for utilities, but they allow students to choose how they want to spend and save their money.
