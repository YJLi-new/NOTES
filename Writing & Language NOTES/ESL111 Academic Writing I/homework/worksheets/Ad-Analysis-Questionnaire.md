5.  Based on your answers to the previous questions, is this commercial effective? Give specific reasons for your answer.

> This commercial is very effective because its pathos is very strong.
>
> First, the ad employs a humorous storyline. The main character claims to be a "model prisoner," but the footage shows him fighting and attempting to escape in prison, which blatantly contradicts his statements and creates a strong contrast and comedic effect. Then, he claims to have become a "new man" through a rehabilitation program, but the activities shown—petting dogs, kneading dough, and shaking hands with other inmates—seem superficial and unrealistic, further enhancing the humor. Finally, when he is unexpectedly granted parole, he directly expresses his surprise, and this unexpected reaction draws the audience's interest once again.
>
> Second, the use of music and sound effects enhances the emotional appeal. The ad consistently uses light, upbeat drumbeat music to create a relaxed and pleasant atmosphere, complementing the humorous content and making the audience feel at ease and entertained. When the main character awaits the parole decision, the music suddenly stops, adding tension and unease, which immerses the audience further.
>
> Third, the ad’s narrative structure is clever, with a well-organized storyline. Through the contrast between the main character's self-statements and his actual behavior, and the sharp contrast between his attitude and the outcome of his parole hearing, the ad systematically builds surprising plot developments, successfully leaving a strong impression on the audience and arousing their interest.
>
> Although the ad lacks logos and ethos, this does not significantly impact its persuasiveness. For an entertainment product like a video game DLC, the audience is more concerned about the game's fun and experience. The ad effectively showcases the game’s features through emotions and humor, sparking the audience’s curiosity and interest, thereby achieving its promotional goal. Factual data, statistics, expert recommendations, and the brand's positive image are less relevant to the needs of the target audience.
>
> In conclusion, while the ad lacks logos and ethos, its strong pathos engages the target audience and sparks interest in the product. As an ad for an entertainment product—a video game DLC—I believe it is effective.
