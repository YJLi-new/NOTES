**What are the functions and characteristics of the following parts of Introduction Paragraphs? Write H, BI or TS on the lines.**

**H** The Hook **BI** Background Information **TS** Thesis Statement

\_TS\_ gives the reader a “preview” of the major points.

\_H\_ is used to get the reader's attention.

\_TS\_ clearly states the purpose or goal of your writing *(in this case, your stance).*

\_TS\_ moves from general to specific.

\_TS\_ responds to the prompt given by the instructor.

\_BI\_ shows that there is a difference of opinion on the topic.

\_BI\_ mentions the article used, including when the article was written.

\_H\_ uses some of these strategies:

> **A startling statistic or unusual fact**
>
> **A vivid example**
>
> **A quotation**
>
> **An description or an image**
>
> **A question that gets your reader thinking**

\_TS\_ is very direct *(don’t make your reader guess what you’re writing about!).*

\_BI\_ shows why the topic is important.

Adapted from Writing Effective Introduction Paragraphs PPT by Jeff Arrigo. Fall 2013
