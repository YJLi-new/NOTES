Step 3: Working Thesis Statement

Come up with a working thesis that shares your STANCE on this issue (yes/no) and gives a PREVIEW with two supporting points.

In my viewpoint, high school students should be allowed to take mental health days off, as it satisfies the mental health needs of students that take a break from studying or working, and gives students an opportunity to meet their social needs without relying on school, away from abuse and neglect.
