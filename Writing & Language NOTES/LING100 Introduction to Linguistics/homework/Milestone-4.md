**Problem C**

Consider Section 2 of the corpus. Provide evidence for every answer.

1\. What is the morpheme for "from" in Korean?

-eyse.

> Evidence:
>
> "I came from Canada" → "Nanun Canadaeyse wassta"
>
> "They came from Canada" → "Kutulun Canadaeyse wassta"
>
> "She came from her house" → "Kunyenun kunye cipeyse wassta"
>
> "I came from my house" → "Nanun nay cipeyse wassta"

2\. What is the morpheme for "in" in Korean?

-eyse.

> Evidence:
>
> "We studied in the library" → "Wulinun tosekwaneyse kongpwuhayssta"
>
> "They studied in the library" → "Kutulun tosekwaneyse kongpwuhay"
>
> "She ate in the bedroom" → "Kunyenun chimsileyse mekessta"

3\. Where do the adpositions ("from" and "in" in these sentences) go in Korean in relation to the noun?

The adposition (“-eyse” in these sentences) goes after the noun.

Evidence:\
"Canadaeyse", "cipeyse", "tosekwaneyse", "chimsileyse"

4\. Provide a narrow IPA transcription in IPA (based on your audio recorded corpus) for the morpheme for "library" in Korean.

\[tosʌɡwan\]

Evidence:

> "Wulinun tosekwaneyse kongpwuhayssta" → "We studied in the library".
>
> “library” = “tosekwan”

**Problem E**

Consider Section 4 of the corpus. Provide evidence for every answer.

1\. Provide a narrow transcription in IPA (based on your audio recorded corpus) for Sentence \#32 - "He climbed the short tree" in Korean

\[kɯnɯn tʃaɡɯn namuɾɯɭ olːaɡas͈t͈a\]

Evidence:

"He climbed the short tree" → "Kunun cakun namwulul ollakassta"

2\. Relative to subject and verb, where do adjectives go in Korean sentences?

Adjectives appear after the subject and before the noun they modify, and the nouns appear before the verbs.

Evidence:

"Kunyenun ppalkan cipul senthaykhayssta" (She - red - house - chose)

> "Kunun cakun namwulul ollakassta" (He - short - tree - climbed)

3\. Relative to subject and verb, where do adverbs go in Korean sentences?

Adverbs appear after the subject and before the verb they modify.

Evidence:

"Nanun ecey syawehayssta" (I - yesterday - showered)

> "Nanun cinan cwuey syawehayssta" (I - last week - showered)

4\. If a sentence has both an adjective and an adverb, what is the relative ordering of them?

Subject - Adverb - Adjective - Noun - Verb.

Evidence:

“Nanun ecey ppalkan sakwalul mekessta" (I - yesterday - red - apple - ate)
