Writing - Summary&Response

In the speech *How I gave up processed foods*, Megan Kimble mainly talks about three processes of processed food, and use them to explain why she gave up and we should give up processed food. The three processes are namely getting food from its source, physical response, and community impact.

The first process refers to a comparison of two forms of processing food. Mechanized farming involves in the mechanization of seeding, harvesting, transportation and storage with long distance while Community Supported Agriculture (CSA) refers to local farming with less storage and no pesticides. The second is about how human’s digestive system respond to the typical ingredient, sugar, from unprocessed food better than from processed food. The third talks about the sustainable development of local economy, environment, and politics under the influence of processed food.

Basicly, I’m agree with Kimble’s opinion in making our food less processed. However, it’s nearly impossible for all of us to avoid every processed food, and the society still needs the food processing industry to promote its economy.Therefore, to protect our health and the sustainable development of food processing industry, individuals, government and society should make joint effort to reduce the harm to both human and society.

As for individuals, firstly, consumers should show the trust and respect to people who grow and harvest food. In this way, business cooperation between buyers and farmers can be easily built. Also, getting to know the ingredients and side effects of food preservatives is important for changing the habit of buying processed food. Besides, people can try to avoid eating high calories food like bananas, chocolates and sea food. Instead, people should purchase fresh food such as vegetables, peanuts, and fresh dairy product, so that our bodies could fully absorb the sugar and nutrients from green food. With adequate time, everyone is recommended to cook themselves, which is healthier, more environmental and more economic, rather than get takeaways.

Beyond a simply personal issue, food safety is also a social issue in terms of its production, distribution and consumption. Thus, the government plays an important role in regulating, monitoring and coordinating the processed food. The government is proposed to establish food regulations and laws with different standards during food processing with comprehensive supervision. Strict laws and regulations may also facilitate to ensure the quality of food produced and sold in the market. What’s more, the government should make effort to educate its citizens about the knowledge of processed food to make them eat to stay healthy. Raising people’s awareness of eating healthilycan be done by broadcasting the harm of having the expired food or over-processed food.

When it comes to processed food safety issue, different parties of the society should show their concern. That benefits the food safety supervision. Social media can broadcast news about different processed food safety cases to alert people to be more cautious about the quality of processed food . Besides, farmers should shoulder the responsibility of producing healthy unprocessed food through using less pesticides, since the application of advanced food technology during processing may cause health risks. Besides, more researches on genetically modified organism (GMO) food, antibiotic, and hormone are needed since people may inevitably consume them. Considering this, food safety researches are essential for people to make food choices.

In conclusion, safety problem of processed food is greatly important since it carries long-term endeavor from individuals, government, as well as our society to ensure the quality of processed food so as to reduce risks in health, environment and economy.
