So far, what I have learned in the course has been very helpful. I have gained a deeper understanding of the cultural differences and identity challenges that international students may face when studying abroad. This has made me reflect on my own experience as an international student and realize that a liberal arts education can cultivate a well-rounded perspective. In addition, the group projects in this course are a great way to connect with other international freshmen. I have benefited greatly from the collaboration and teamwork, which has enhanced my ability to work effectively in a team.

One of my favorite textbooks is I'm Korean but Culturally Black by Cindy Wilson. As a child adopted in South Korea and raised in an African American household, I was fascinated by her unique experience of dual identities. The book provides valuable insights into how identity is influenced by culture, and made me think more deeply about the complexity of identity in a multicultural world. It made me more aware of the importance of self-discovery.

As described in “The Core of Higher Education,” I found the intersection of life and learning in my programming studies. Immediately after completing the computer science course, I applied what I had learned to write a small program for my personal project. At the same time, I also gained a sense of accomplishment because I not only learned the knowledge required for the exam, but also applied it to the real world, which quickly made my learning experience meaningful.

To further enrich my experience in this course, having more practical, hands-on learning opportunities that connect the course concepts to real-world applications would have been helpful. For example, the instructor could have increased the frequency of discussions with classmates to facilitate the exchange of different perspectives. By increasing experiential learning and discussions, this course could provide deeper and more relevant content for our academic and personal growth.
