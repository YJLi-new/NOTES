## Q1

$n = \frac{2 - 0}{0.25} = 8$; let $x_0 = 0, x_1 = 0.25, \dots, x_7 = 1.75, x_8 = 2$.

**a. (Composite trapezoidal)**

$$\int_0^2 x^2\ln(x^2 + 1)\,dx \approx \frac{0.25}{2}\Big[0 + 2\Big(\frac{\ln 17}{16} - \frac{\ln 2}{4} + \frac{\ln 5}{4} - \frac{\ln 2}{2} + \frac98\ln 5 - \frac94\ln 2 + \ln 2 + \frac{25}{16}\ln 41 - \frac{25}{4}\ln 2 + \frac94\ln 13 - \frac92\ln 2 + \frac{49}{16}\ln 65 - \frac{49}{4}\ln 2\Big) + 4\ln 5\Big] \approx 3.1595$$

**b. (Composite Simpson)**

$$\int_0^2 x^2\ln(x^2 + 1)\,dx \approx \frac{0.25}{3}\Big[0 + 4\Big(\frac{\ln 17}{16} - \frac{\ln 2}{4} + \frac98\ln 5 - \frac94\ln 2 + \frac{25}{16}\ln 41 - \frac{25}{4}\ln 2 + \frac{49}{16}\ln 65 - \frac{49}{4}\ln 2\Big) + 2\Big(\frac{\ln 5}{4} - \frac{\ln 2}{2} + \ln 2 + \frac94\ln 13 - \frac92\ln 2\Big) + 4\ln 5\Big] \approx 3.1093$$

$$\int_0^2 x^2\ln(x^2 + 1)\,dx \approx 0.25\sum_{i=1}^8\Big\{\Big(0.25i - \frac{0.25}{2}\Big)^2\ln\Big[\Big(0.25i - \frac{0.25}{2}\Big)^2 + 1\Big]\Big\} \approx 3.0842$$
